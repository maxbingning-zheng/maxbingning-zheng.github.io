隐私政策

生效日期：2026-01-01

本隐私政策适用于郑勇飞（Max Zheng）开发的以下应用：球道AKB（以下简称“AKB”）与 SnookerPad。本政策说明这两个应用如何处理您的信息。

第一部分：球道AKB（AKB）

1. 信息收集与存储

AKB 是一款本地桌球计分应用。您在应用中创建的所有数据——包括比赛记录、选手名称、计分数据、成长卡片数据、应用设置——均存储在您的设备本地。

若您启用 iCloud 同步，上述数据会通过 Apple 的 iCloud 服务，在您使用同一 Apple ID 登录的设备之间同步。我们没有自己的服务器，不收集、不存储、不访问您的任何比赛数据。iCloud 数据的存储与安全，受 Apple 的 iCloud 服务条款和隐私政策约束。

AKB 集成了 Firebase Crashlytics，用于收集应用崩溃信息，帮助我们定位和修复问题。该服务可能收集设备型号、操作系统版本、崩溃时的运行状态等技术信息，不收集您的个人身份信息或比赛数据。

2. 信息使用

  • 本地数据：仅用于实现应用自身功能。
  • iCloud 同步数据：仅用于在您的多台设备间同步数据。
  • 崩溃日志：仅用于诊断和修复技术问题。

3. 信息共享

除以下情形外，我们不会与任何第三方共享您的信息：

  • Apple iCloud：仅在您启用同步时使用。
  • Firebase Crashlytics：仅传输崩溃相关技术信息。

4. 广告与内购

AKB 不包含任何广告。

AKB 提供付费解锁功能。用户下载后可享受一段完整功能体验期，体验期结束后，成长卡片模块需通过应用内购买解锁，以获得永久使用权。所有支付均通过 Apple App Store 完成，我们不接触或存储您的支付信息。

5. 数据删除

由于数据存储于您的设备本地及您的 iCloud 账户，您可以随时通过卸载应用或删除 iCloud 数据来清除相关信息。如有疑问，可通过下方邮箱联系我们。

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

This Privacy Policy applies to the following apps developed by Max Zheng: AKB and SnookerPad. It explains how these two apps handle your information.

Part 1: AKB

1. Information Collection and Storage

AKB is a local billiards scoring app. All data you create in the app—including match records, player names, scoring data, growth card data, and app settings—is stored locally on your device.

If you enable iCloud sync, this data is synchronized across your devices signed in with the same Apple ID through Apple's iCloud service. We do not operate our own servers and do not collect, store, or access any of your match data. The storage and security of iCloud data are governed by Apple's iCloud Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

AKB integrates Firebase Crashlytics to collect app crash information, helping us identify and fix issues. This service may collect technical information such as device model, OS version, and runtime state at the time of a crash. It does not collect your personal identity information or match data.

2. How We Use Information

  • Local data: used solely to provide the app's own features.
  • iCloud sync data: used solely to synchronize data across your devices.
  • Crash logs: used solely to diagnose and fix technical issues.

3. Information Sharing

We do not share your information with any third party, except in the following cases:

  • Apple iCloud: only when you enable sync.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: only technical crash-related information is transmitted.

4. Advertising and In-App Purchases

AKB contains no advertising.

AKB offers a paid unlock feature. Users can enjoy a full-featured trial period after download. After the trial period ends, the Growth Card module requires an In-App Purchase to unlock permanent access. All payments are processed through the Apple App Store, and we do not access or store your payment information.

5. Data Deletion

Since data is stored locally on your device and in your iCloud account, you can clear it at any time by uninstalling the app or deleting iCloud data. If you have questions, please contact us via the email below.
